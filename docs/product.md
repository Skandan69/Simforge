# SimForge product

## Mission

SimForge is an **AI Workforce Intelligence Platform**, not an LMS. Its north star is measurable capability improvement after practice and assessment.

## Core loop

1. Capture organization priorities in the Organization Blueprint.
2. Upload governed company knowledge once.
3. Process, classify, chunk, embed and retrieve that knowledge.
4. Generate review-required learning assets in Learning Factory.
5. Publish approved simulations into Simulation Studio.
6. Run Sophia roleplay grounded in the organization context.
7. Evaluate performance and create capability evidence.
8. Generate AI Coach feedback and update Capability Profiles.
9. Give managers team intelligence and targeted assignment controls.
10. Route learners through Practice, Assessments and Development Paths.
11. Report evidence and repeat the loop to show improvement over time.

## Primary users

- **Owner/Admin** — workspace, members, governance and oversight.
- **Trainer** — knowledge, simulations, assessments and development assets.
- **Manager** — team intelligence, assignments and development follow-up.
- **Learner** — practice, assessments, coaching, capability profile and development paths.

## Pilot-ready capability set

- authentication and organization workspaces
- workspace invitations and role management
- Organization Blueprint
- Knowledge Studio + processing + intelligence
- retrieval-grounded Ask Sophia
- Learning Factory → Simulation publishing
- Sophia text/voice simulations
- deterministic/AI evaluation with capability scoring
- AI Coach
- Capability Profile/history
- Manager Intelligence
- Practice assignments + My Practice
- Assessment Studio + My Assessments
- Development Paths + My Development
- evidence-based Reports and CSV capability export
- tenant/security hardening, rate limits and health diagnostics

## Product boundaries

Pilot scope deliberately excludes a generic LMS/course engine, SCORM, attendance, marketplace/catalog functionality, adaptive-path automation, deep HRIS integrations, and broad executive BI. Those should only be added after customer evidence demonstrates a need.

## Definition of guided enterprise pilot ready

A customer can:

- create a workspace and add the pilot team,
- upload real governed knowledge,
- create/publish a grounded simulation,
- run a learner through Sophia,
- evaluate and coach the learner,
- assign practice/assessment/development work,
- see capability change and team evidence,
- export a basic capability report,
- operate without cross-tenant leakage or direct client access to API-owned tables.

Future work should be driven by pilot evidence rather than continuing an automatic sprint sequence.
